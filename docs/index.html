Features

Separate import/export integration, current-quarter budgets, instant and smoothed projections, and sustained-horizon advice.

Permanent archive

Integration-owned SQLite quarter summaries with Forever retention, immutable historical targets, and canonical UTC identities.

Five tariff blocks

Versioned Slovenian B1–B5 calendars and dated, user-configured contracted-power assignments.

HISTORY

Installation

  1. Add this repository as a HACS custom integration repository.
  2. Install SI Grid Monitor and restart Home Assistant.
  3. Add the integration and select one signed W or kW grid-power sensor.
  4. Confirm polarity and enter dated B1–B5 contracted-power assignments.

Updates preserve config-entry identities and the SQLite archive. Restart Home Assistant and hard-refresh the browser after frontend updates; never delete the integration to clear a browser cache.

Dashboard

The automatically registered sidebar panel contains Overview, Today, Period, Blocks, Exceedances, History and Tariff Calendar. No Lovelace YAML or manual JavaScript resource is required.

CAN ADD is mathematical 15-minute imported-energy headroom. It is not permission to exceed breaker, phase, wiring, inverter or connection limits.

History and Recorder audit

Archive rows contain completed-quarter summaries, not historical power traces. HISTORY never invents a detailed curve. User-initiated Recorder audits may inspect detailed states in bounded ranges. Controlled reconstruction requires a dry run, explicit confirmation and a SQLite backup; stronger records are never replaced by weaker evidence.

Recorder-derived values are sample-derived estimates and may differ from distributor-meter measurements. Long-term hourly statistics cannot recover separate quarter import and export.

Tariff rules

2026 and 2027 rules are marked official from the Slovenian Energy Agency (URO). 2028–2030 are explicitly provisional continuations and disabled unless the user opts in. Bundled rules work offline; unreviewed remote rules never become active automatically.

Data quality

Measured-aligned, counter-derived, reconstructed, incomplete, unavailable, unknown-configuration, provisional and simulation states remain distinct. Missing energy is never treated as zero. Confirmed exceedances require complete eligible measurement, known configuration, an official rule and the original effective target.

FAQ and troubleshooting

Why is advice unavailable?

The source may be stale, coverage insufficient, the tariff unverified, or no dated contracted-power assignment may cover the interval. Physical metering and archive recording continue.

Does export cancel earlier import?

No. Contractual demand uses imported energy only. Exported energy is measured separately.

Where is the database?

It is integration-owned storage inside Home Assistant configuration storage. Use supported backup operations; copying a live WAL database manually may be inconsistent.
