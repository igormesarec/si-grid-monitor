# SI Grid Monitor V1.0.3 implementation status

Status: **draft release candidate in PR #19; not released or accepted on a real
Home Assistant installation.**

## Implemented

- Existing V1.0.2 schema-7 contracts, audit, Repairs, calendar evidence and
  archive behavior are preserved.
- English/Slovenian installation preference and persistent dashboard override.
- One centralized publishing controller separates one-second calculation from
  Home Assistant entity state publication.
- Explicit publication policy covers every sensor and binary sensor without
  changing entity keys or unique IDs.
- Storage Optimized default, Automation Priority, Custom and Restore Defaults
  OptionsFlow paths in English and Slovenian.
- Immediate unsafe critical activation with a 30-second continuously-safe
  projected-exceedance clear; direction-aware actionable escalation (MUST
  REDUCE increases and CAN ADD decreases), plus continuously-safe debounce for
  actionable clears/headroom recovery; configurable intervals/deadbands;
  no stable-state or wall-clock-attribute heartbeat.
- Natural source staleness makes projected exceedance unavailable just after
  the 60-second evidence window. Current/import/export power and every
  instantaneous-dependent numeric forecast/advice entity also become
  HA-unavailable on that same evaluation; UNKNOWN is never published as a safe
  false or an old optimistic number. Static contract targets and already
  evidenced partial energy remain visible.
- Availability recovery and nonzero optimistic CAN ADD/MUST REDUCE improvement
  require 30 seconds of uninterrupted healthy evidence. Nonzero improvements
  then retain the configured actionable cadence, avoiding a new 30-second
  publication heartbeat; safety deterioration remains prompt when material.
- A reader/serialization/policy/callback failure is isolated per entity. A
  bounded fault callback attempts to replace stale public advice with
  unavailable, exposes faulted keys in diagnostics and retries at most every 30
  seconds if the HA write itself fails.
- Current-quarter imported and known exported energy publish after the first
  minute, within a 180-second bound thereafter, and reset on the canonical
  quarter boundary. Mode C export remains unavailable; completed archive
  precision remains authoritative.
- Completed-quarter demand and signed-average sensors carry canonical UTC
  start/end identity, so equal consecutive values produce one event per quarter
  without a heartbeat. Archive-restored identity remains stable across restart.
- Diagnostic event-history truncation is evaluated per hour/day/week window and
  recovers after discarded events leave that window; partial startup evidence
  is never annualized as a complete window.
- Resetting current-quarter import/export energy entities no longer advertise
  the lifetime-total state class. Their entity identities and values are
  unchanged and no Recorder history is rewritten.
- Reduced dynamic entity attributes plus Home Assistant unrecorded-attribute
  declarations where diagnostic attributes remain useful.
- Authenticated, entry-namespaced Data Publishing & Storage diagnostics with
  bounded one-hour/day/week windows and all-meter aggregation.
- Responsive Contracts status, historical evidence and audit cards; desktop
  tables remain available.
- Tariff Calendar scroll/selection ownership and explicit offsets for the two
  02:00 hours on the 2026 autumn DST transition.

## Storage benchmark

The deterministic test runs complete Europe/Ljubljana civil days for two
meters through real sensor/binary-sensor values, availability and standard plus
integration attributes. The Recorder-like model includes shared attributes,
metadata/timestamp/context fields and three indexes.

| Local day | Seconds / quarters | Rows | SQLite bytes/day | Recorder-like annual | Unverified Influx allowance | Combined with archive |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Spring DST, 29 Mar 2026 | 82,800 / 92 | 6,912 | 1,286,144 | 489.9 MB | 368.6 MB | 938.4 MB |
| Normal, 2 Apr 2026 | 86,400 / 96 | 7,196 | 1,286,144 | 469.4 MB | 367.7 MB | 917.2 MB |
| Autumn DST, 25 Oct 2026 | 90,000 / 100 | 7,512 | 1,400,832 | 490.9 MB | 368.5 MB | 939.4 MB |

InfluxDB is estimated at 140 bytes per emitted event only for planning. CI does
not run InfluxDB 1.x; physical InfluxDB storage and the combined <=1 GB target
remain **unverified** until isolated 24-hour real-instance measurement. These
figures are planning evidence, not physical-storage acceptance.

## Remaining manual acceptance

- Clean and upgraded Home Assistant instances with two real A/B/C meters.
- Android Companion at 320/360/390/430 px and desktop/tablet layouts, both
  themes and both languages.
- Native OptionsFlow profile persistence and automation behavior.
- Real Home Assistant entity availability and Recorder statistics behavior
  after removal of the inappropriate resetting-energy `total` state class.
- A deliberately injected HA state-write failure to confirm logs/diagnostics
  remain actionable when the fallback unavailable write itself cannot succeed.
- A real 24-hour Recorder/InfluxDB event-rate and allocated-storage measurement.
- Careful removal of temporary exclusions only after validation; separate
  backup-first cleanup of legacy rows if the administrator chooses.

No automatic legacy cleanup, Recorder configuration edit, InfluxDB retention
change, schema migration, archive rewrite, merge, tag or release is included.
