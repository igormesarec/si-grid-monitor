## PR #5 review corrections

- Setup and Options: translated `data_description` now provides field-by-field explanations for every input, in addition to step summaries (English and Slovenian).
- TODAY chart: a shared kW scale places import-only demand above a fixed zero line, average export below it, and immutable quarter targets on a stepped line. It distinguishes measured zero, missing, incomplete, provisional, live and confirmed data.
- TODAY data: the in-progress quarter is overlaid from runtime measurements and its projection is never counted as a completed result. Future current-day intervals are UPCOMING. Block summaries use the same archive validity classifier as daily totals, exclude simulations from genuine statistics and keep partial observations separate.
- Multiple meters: changing entry clears live, historical, Today, calendar and transition state; entry/generation tokens reject delayed responses from the previous meter.
- Archive navigation: Previous/Next use exact UTC 15-minute steps, repeated local times include their UTC offsets, future archive navigation is blocked, and summary-only records never fabricate traces.
- TARIFF CALENDAR: selectable hourly cells display block, effective kW target, rule verification, holiday, timezone offset and DST fold.
- Upcoming lower-limit panel: separates horizon-wide sustained reduction from an informational future contract change; reports unavailable forecasts honestly rather than attributing a reduction to the next block without evidence.
- Python view-model and Node behavioral tests cover archive reconciliation, live-quarter rollover, DST day lengths, graph geometry, missing versus measured zero, navigation and stale multi-meter responses. Real HA/HACS visual and interaction validation remains required. SQLite stays at schema 5 and no source epoch or historical row is rewritten.
